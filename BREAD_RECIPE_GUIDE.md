# Bread Making Guide - Solving the "Won't Rise" Problem

## 🔍 Problem Diagnosis

### Why Isn't Your Bread Rising?

**Main Reason: Missing Yeast!**

- ❌ Baking powder & baking soda: Good for cakes, cookies, quick breads
- ✅ **Yeast**: Essential for toast-style bread! This is the key to making bread rise

Traditional toast bread requires yeast fermentation to become fluffy and tall.

---

## 📝 Solution 1: Quick Bread (Using Available Ingredients)

Since you don't have yeast, we can make **quick bread** (similar to pound cake texture, but more bread-like)

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| All-purpose flour | 300g |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Milk | 180ml |
| Sugar | 40g |
| Salt | 5g |
| Baking powder | 12g (about 3 tsp) |
| Baking soda | 3g (about 1/2 tsp) |
| Oil/melted butter | 30g (if available) |

### Instructions

#### 1️⃣ Preparation (5 minutes)
```
1. Preheat oven/air fryer to 170°C (340°F)
2. Prepare a loaf pan or baking dish, grease to prevent sticking
```

#### 2️⃣ Mix Dry Ingredients
```
In a large bowl, mix:
- Flour 300g
- Sugar 40g
- Salt 5g
- Baking powder 12g
- Baking soda 3g

Stir thoroughly, sifting is even better!
```

#### 3️⃣ Mix Wet Ingredients
```
In another bowl, whisk together:
- Eggs 2
- Milk 180ml
- Oil 30g (if available)

Stir until fully combined
```

#### 4️⃣ Combine Batter
```
⚠️ Critical Step:
1. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients
2. Gently fold with a rubber spatula
3. ❌ Don't overmix! Stop when no dry flour is visible
4. Small lumps in the batter are normal
5. ✅ Overmixing will make bread tough and dense
```

#### 5️⃣ Pan and Rest
```
1. Pour batter into pan, fill about 70% full
2. ⏰ Rest for 15-20 minutes
   (Baking powder will start creating bubbles, batter will slightly expand)
3. Gently tap pan to release large air bubbles
```

#### 6️⃣ Air Fryer Baking
```
Temperature and time settings:
- Stage 1: 160°C (320°F), bake 15 minutes
- Stage 2: Reduce to 140°C (285°F), bake another 20-25 minutes

⚠️ Important Tips:
- Don't open frequently (will deflate)
- Absolutely don't open for the first 20 minutes!
- Insert toothpick in center, comes out clean = done

🔥 If surface browns too quickly:
- Cover with foil after 15 minutes
```

#### 7️⃣ Cooling
```
1. Remove from pan immediately after baking
2. Place on cooling rack
3. ⏰ Wait until completely cool before slicing (at least 1 hour)
   Cutting while hot will cause collapse!
```

## 📝 Solution 2: Traditional Toast Bread (Requires Yeast)

If you want to make real tall toast, **you must buy yeast!**

### Materials to Purchase
- **Active Dry Yeast** or **Instant Yeast**: 5g

### Toast Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Bread flour | 300g |
| Milk (warm 35-40°C) | 180ml |
| Yeast | 5g |
| Sugar | 30g |
| Salt | 5g |
| Egg | 1 |
| Butter/oil | 30g |

### Instructions

#### 1️⃣ Activate Yeast
```
1. Warm milk (35-40°C / 95-104°F, should feel warm to touch)
2. Add 1 tsp sugar
3. Sprinkle yeast, stir
4. Rest 5-10 minutes, should see foam on surface
```

#### 2️⃣ Knead Dough
```
1. Mix flour, sugar, salt
2. Add yeast milk mixture, egg
3. Knead into smooth dough (15-20 minutes)
4. Finally add softened butter, continue kneading until windowpane stage
```

#### 3️⃣ First Rise
```
⏰ Critical step!
1. Place dough in warm spot (28-30°C / 82-86°F)
2. Cover with damp cloth
3. Rise for 1-1.5 hours
4. Dough should double in size
5. Poke with finger, if it doesn't spring back = ready
```

#### 4️⃣ Punch Down and Shape
```
1. Gently press dough to deflate
2. Divide into 3 portions
3. Roll into balls, rest 15 minutes
4. Roll out into rectangles
5. Roll up and place in loaf pan
```

#### 5️⃣ Second Rise
```
⏰ Key to tall bread!
1. Place in warm, humid spot
2. Rise 40-60 minutes
3. Should reach 80-90% of pan height
```

#### 6️⃣ Baking
```
Air fryer settings:
- Preheat to 180°C (355°F)
- Bake 25-30 minutes
- Surface should be golden brown

⚠️ If surface browns too quickly, cover with foil
```

## 💡 Key Tips for Tall Bread

### Using Baking Powder (Quick Bread):
1. ✅ **Fresh baking powder**: Expired won't work
   - Test: 1 tsp baking powder + hot water, should bubble immediately
2. ✅ **Don't overmix**: Will break all the bubbles
3. ✅ **Bake immediately after pouring**: Baking powder reaction will fade
4. ✅ **Don't overfill pan**: 70% full is best
5. ✅ **Don't open during baking**: Absolutely not for first 20 minutes!

### Using Yeast (Traditional Bread):
1. ✅ **Correct water temperature**: 35-40°C (too hot kills yeast)
2. ✅ **Warm proofing environment**: 28-30°C optimal
3. ✅ **Sufficient proofing time**: Can't rush
4. ✅ **Knead properly**: Should reach windowpane stage
5. ✅ **Second rise is important**: This is the key to height!

## 🔧 Air Fryer Tips

### Temperature Adjustment
```
Air fryers run hotter than regular ovens, suggest:
- Recipe temperature -10°C to -20°C
- Example: 180°C → 160-170°C
```

### Prevent Burning
```
1. Cover bread with foil
2. Rotate pan midway for even heating
3. Place a pan underneath to catch drips
```

### Test for Doneness
```
1. Toothpick inserted in center comes out clean → done
2. Tap bottom, sounds hollow → done
3. Internal temperature reaches 90-95°C (195-203°F) → perfect
```

## ❓ FAQ

### Q1: My quick bread still isn't tall enough?
**A:** Check these:
- Is baking powder fresh?
- Did you overmix?
- Did you open the air fryer during baking?
- Is the pan too large (batter should be 70% full)?

### Q2: Can I omit eggs?
**A:** Yes, but it will affect fluffiness
- Replace 1 egg with 50ml milk
- Add 1g extra baking powder to compensate

### Q3: Can I skip oil/butter?
**A:** Yes, but texture will be drier
- Quick bread can skip oil
- Traditional toast strongly recommend including it

### Q4: Can I use sweet potato starch?
**A:** Yes, can replace part of the flour
- Use 30-50g sweet potato starch for equal amount of flour
- Will make bread softer

- ✅ Texture: Like pound cake, dense but soft

### Yeast Toast:
- ✅ Height: 2-3x taller than dough
- ✅ Surface: Golden brown, smooth
- ✅ Texture: Clear pull-apart strands, fluffy and soft
